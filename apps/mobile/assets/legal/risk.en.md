# Risk Disclosure

> Draft — must be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before public launch.

Last updated: September 2026

Trading crypto assets, stock tokens and derivatives involves significant risk. Only trade with money you can afford to lose. Read this disclosure carefully.

## Crypto assets are volatile

Prices can rise or fall sharply within minutes. Markets run 24/7 and can move while you are asleep. You may lose some or all of your money.

## Memecoins are especially risky

Memecoins often have little liquidity, concentrated ownership and no underlying value. They are vulnerable to sudden crashes, rug pulls and manipulation. Their price can go to zero.

## Leverage and liquidation

Perpetual futures let you trade with leverage. Leverage magnifies both gains and losses. If the price moves against you, your position can be liquidated automatically and you will lose the margin you posted. Liquidation prices shown in the app are estimates; the actual liquidation price may differ because of fees, funding and market conditions. Funding payments can reduce your margin over time.

## Stock tokens are not shares

Stock tokens provide price exposure to a company's shares. They are tokenized exposure, not direct share ownership. They may not include voting rights, dividends or the investor protections that apply to shares held with a regulated broker. Availability varies by region. Prices outside regular market hours may differ from the underlying market.

## Copy trading and social content

Copying other traders does not guarantee similar results. Their past performance, leaderboard rank or thesis accuracy does not predict future returns. Posts and theses are opinions, not advice.

## Technology and counterparty risk

Blockchains, wallets, smart contracts and third-party venues can fail, be hacked or become unavailable. Transactions are usually irreversible. Sending assets on the wrong network can lead to permanent loss.

## Regulatory risk

Laws governing crypto assets and derivatives change. Features may become unavailable in your region.

## Demo mode

In demo mode everything is simulated. Demo results do not indicate how you would perform with real money.
