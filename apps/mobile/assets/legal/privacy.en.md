# Privacy Policy

> Draft — must be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before public launch.

Last updated: September 2026

## 1. Who we are

This policy explains how hopium.family ("we") collects, uses and shares personal data when you use our apps and website.

## 2. Data we collect

- **Account data:** email address, sign-in method, username, display name, bio, avatar, country and birth year.
- **Activity data:** trades, orders, positions, theses, posts, comments, likes, follows, notification settings and price alerts.
- **Wallet data:** public wallet addresses and balances. We never collect private keys or seed phrases.
- **Verification data:** where identity verification is required, our KYC partner collects identity documents directly. We receive only the result.
- **Device data:** push notification tokens, app version, language and basic diagnostics.

We do not collect card numbers. Payments are handled directly by our on-ramp partners.

## 3. How we use data

- To provide the Service, including feeds, leaderboards, notifications and order execution.
- To keep the Service safe: moderation, fraud prevention and detecting market manipulation such as wash trading.
- To meet legal and regulatory obligations, including region restrictions and KYC.
- To improve the Service using aggregated analytics.

## 4. What others can see

Your username, profile, public trades and theses are visible to other users. By default, trade sizes are shown as ranges, not exact amounts, unless you choose to share exact amounts. You can make your holdings private in Settings. Blocked users cannot see your content.

## 5. Sharing

We share data with service providers who help us run the Service (hosting, wallets, market data, on-ramp, KYC, analytics and error monitoring), under contracts that protect your data. We share data with authorities when required by law. We do not sell your personal data.

## 6. International transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards.

## 7. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your profile and content. Trade records may be retained in anonymized form where the law requires it.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your data and to object to certain processing. You can delete your account in Settings or contact support@hopium.family.

## 9. Security

We use encryption in transit, row-level access controls and secure storage for session tokens. No system is perfectly secure.

## 10. Children

The Service is only for adults 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

## 11. Changes and contact

We will notify you in the app of material changes. Contact: support@hopium.family.
